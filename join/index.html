Ten bucks. Founding member.

Joining any chartered Valley club is the same $10 ASO semester fee. Join this one while it's being founded and your name is on the record from day one. No coding experience required, no application, no interview.

Three steps

STEP ONE

Pay the $10 ASO fee

At the LAVC Business Office (one fee per semester, and it covers every club you join plus ASO member benefits).

STEP TWO

Register on the ASU form

Use the official Club Member Sign-Up & Officer Registration form and select Computational Biology and Pre-Med Society. Student login required. If the club isn't in the drop-down yet, ASU is still processing our listing: check back in a few days or just show up to a meeting.

Show up

First and third Tuesday, 6:30–7:30 PM, hybrid. Kickoff is the first general meeting after the September 24 charter vote: you'll help choose the semester's communal project.
